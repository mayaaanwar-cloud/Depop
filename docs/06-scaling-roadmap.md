# 06 — 12-Month Scaling Roadmap

Falsifiable plan. Every month has exit criteria; the Sunday review tracks actuals.
Revenue figures are cumulative-monthly (all platforms). "Listings" = active listings live.

## Phase 1 — LAUNCH (Months 1–2)
**Goal: build the machine, prove the pipeline.**

- Set up shop (brand from doc 07), photo station, pack station, ledger
- Source: 2 bin trips + 2 thrift circuits/month + first 45lb bale in M2
- List 4–6/day from day one → **150–250 active listings by end of M2**
- Depop only (learn one platform's rhythm first)
- Social: account live, 3 posts/wk documenting drops
- **Exit criteria:** 200+ listings · 60+ total sales · ASP ≥ $28 · ship time <24h ·
  revenue $1.5–3k/mo

## Phase 2 — CROSS-LIST (Months 3–4)
**Goal: multiply every listing's surface area.**

- Vendoo live; back-catalog cross-listed to eBay + Poshmark + Mercari in week 1 of M3
- Sourcing: +1 bale/month standing order; premium-tier share to 25%
- Offer waves + markdown cadence fully running
- **350–450 active listings**
- **Exit criteria:** 4 platforms live · sell-through ≥30% · ASP ≥ $35 · revenue $4–6k/mo

## Phase 3 — SCALE SOURCING (Months 5–8)
**Goal: volume + ASP ladder.**

- Negotiate recurring bale contract (2×45lb/mo, LTL if 200lb+) — landed COGS down 15–20%
- Premium mix to 30–35% (cashmere, Carhartt, barn jackets, blazers per doc 01 risers)
- Relist engine: everything >30 days cycles automatically per doc 04
- Social flywheel: 5 posts/wk; drops announced to followers ("new drop Fridays")
- **700+ active listings**
- **Exit criteria:** revenue $8–12k/mo · ASP ≥ $42 · 30-day sell-through ≥30% ·
  follower count 5k+

## Phase 4 — OPTIMIZE & DELEGATE (Months 9–12)
**Goal: six-figure run-rate.**

- Shipping help 2×/wk; operator hours shift to sourcing + premium curation
- Cut bottom-quartile categories (ledger data decides, not feelings)
- Premium mix to 40%; ASP $50–60
- Boost aging winners selectively (doc 04 rules)
- **1,000+ active listings**
- **Exit criteria: $12–18k/mo revenue run-rate = $145–215k annualized revenue,
  ~$70–110k annualized profit at ≥65% net margin**

## Beyond month 12 (the real six-figure-profit consolidation)

- Year 2 levers, in order of ROI: (1) hold $15k+/mo for 12 straight months,
  (2) website/Shopify for owned traffic off-platform, (3) live selling (Whatnot) for
  bulk-clearing filler tier, (4) a second niche shop if the brand ceiling is hit
- **$100k+ profit year = ~$180–200k revenue at target margins.** The month-12 machine
  (1,000 listings, 4 platforms, bale contracts, help on shipping) is exactly the machine
  that does that number in year 2 — with room to spare if ASP lands at $60.

## Slippage protocol

Behind plan 1 month → diagnose in Sunday review (volume? ASP? sell-through? — the KPI
table isolates which lever broke). Behind 2 months → strategy session with Claude:
re-comp the market, rebalance categories, audit listing quality against doc 03.
The plan bends; the listing quota doesn't.
