# 07 — Brand Bible

A branded shop compounds: followers → repeat buyers → engagement signals → algorithm boost
→ more followers. A random closet does not.

## 1. Positioning

**The shop:** a tightly-curated vintage + Y2K + elevated-basics shop — "the friend with
insane thrift luck." Two aesthetic poles that cover 2026's demand map:

- **Pole A — Y2K/street/workwear:** baggy denim, Carhartt, vintage Nike, tracksuits,
  graphic tees. High velocity, feeds volume.
- **Pole B — "The Edited Self" minimalist:** cashmere, structured blazers, barn jackets,
  COS-adjacent quiet pieces. High ASP, feeds margin.

One shop can hold both if the *presentation* is unified (same backdrop, same edit, same
voice). The grid is the brand.

## 3. Voice & visual identity

- **Voice:** warm, knowledgeable, zero corporate-speak. Talks like a stylish friend:
  "this chore coat is criminally good." Honest about flaws, always.
- **Visuals:** one backdrop, one preset, natural light. Modeled shots when possible.
  Grid alternates poles A/B so the shop reads "range," not "chaos."
- **Packaging as marketing:** tissue or kraft wrap + handwritten thank-you card with
  handle + "10% off next order" — repeat-buyer engine for ~$0.30/order.

## 4. Social strategy (the free traffic engine)

Platform-native, 3–5 posts/week, batched Fridays:
- **TikTok/Reels formats that work for resellers:** haul videos ("$83 bin haul → priced"),
  before/after steaming, "what sold this week + for how much," styling one piece 3 ways,
  drop announcements
- **Instagram:** grid = best listings; stories = daily drops + polls ("keep or sell?")
- Every video ends: "everything's on my Depop — link in bio"
- **Weekly drop ritual:** "new drop Friday 7pm" trains followers into a habit and
  concentrates first-hour engagement — exactly what the algorithm rewards

## 5. Reputation standards (non-negotiable)

- Every review 5★ or a documented lesson; disclose every flaw with a photo
- <24h ship, <2h message replies during waking hours
- Bundle discount always on; every order slightly over-delivers (card, neat pack)

The brand is the moat. Fees, algorithms, and trends will change; 10,000 followers who
trust the shop will not.
