# 08 — The First $1K Sprint (Days 1–14)

Goal: **$1,000 revenue in the first 14–21 days** and a working pipeline. Speed of cash
comes from listing velocity + pricing slightly under comp on the first 100 items
(we buy reviews and algorithm reputation early; margin ladder comes later).

**First-sale accelerators:** price first 100 items at the 40–50th percentile of solds
(not 60–70th — that starts in week 4). Ship first orders with the thank-you card.
Every early 5★ review compounds the reliability multiplier on all future listings.

## Where to find sourcing spots (do once, Day 1, 15 min)

1. Google Maps: `"Goodwill Outlet" OR "bins" near me` — outlets sell BY THE POUND ($1.50–2.50/lb).
2. Google Maps: `thrift store` — shortlist the 3 biggest by floor area; note their restock days (call and ask).
3. estatesales.net — set alerts for your zip; go on final day (50–75% off).
4. Wholesale bales (month 2): thriftvintagefashion.com, Reed's Raghouse, regional rag houses — 45lb Y2K/denim mix bale ~$200–350.

Send Claude your city → Claude builds the ranked sourcing map with a route.

## The batch photo formula (30 items ≈ 45 min, no decisions)

Fixed: same wall, same tripod height, same time of day (consistent light), phone in
portrait, gridlines on.
- Steam/lint-roll all 30 first (assembly line, ~15 min)
- Per item, 5 shots in 60–90 sec: front → back → label+size tag → detail → flaw (if any)
- Edit: one preset (same exposure/warmth bump), batch-apply
- Rename nothing, sort nothing — just fill the haul sheet as you shoot (next section)

## The haul sheet → listing factory loop (this is the AI engine)

After every shoot, fill `templates/haul-intake-template.md` (60 sec/item) and paste it
to Claude. Claude returns, same day, for every item: comp-based price + title + full
description + all tags, ready to paste into Depop. You never write copy. You never
guess a price. Your only listing job is paste + attach photos: **~2 min/item.**
