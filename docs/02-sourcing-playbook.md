# 02 — Sourcing Playbook

Sourcing is where profit is made. The sale just collects it.
**Golden rule: never pay more than 25% of the conservative resale comp.**

## 1. Channel economics (2026 rates)

| Channel | Cost | Effort | Best for | Phase |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Bin stores / outlets** (Goodwill Outlets etc.) | $1.50–2.50/lb (≈$1–3/item) | High (digging) | Volume filler, mall brands, hidden cashmere/denim | M1+ |
| **Thrift stores** | $4–8/item picked | Medium | Curated pieces, learning comps | M1+ |
| **Wholesale vintage bales** | $2–5/lb; bales $150–800 (45–100lb) | Low per item | Y2K, tees, denim, workwear at scale | M2+ |
| **Rag houses / textile recyclers** | Lowest per lb, ungraded | High (grading/cleaning) | Only after you can grade fast | M6+ |
| **Estate sales / yard sales** | Variable, negotiable | Medium | Vintage grails, cashmere, leather | Opportunistic |
| **Online arbitrage** (eBay lots, FB Marketplace bundles) | Variable | Low | Filling specific gaps (e.g. "barn jackets") | M3+ |

Bulk shipping note: wholesalers use LTL freight for 200lb+ orders — consolidate bale orders
to cut per-item landed cost.

## 2. The buy list (ranked by profit density, July 2026)

**Always buy on sight (if <25% of comp):**
1. Cashmere anything with intact label (comp $60–120; bin cost ~$2)
2. Carhartt — jackets, double-knees, chore coats
3. Levi's 501/505/550, vintage Wranglers, baggy/skater silhouette jeans
4. Barn jackets / waxed canvas / chore coats (searches +3,138%)
5. Vintage Nike (esp. embroidered logo, vintage sport), Oakley, Prada Linea Rossa
6. Band tees, vintage graphic tees (single-stitch = premium)
7. Vintage tracksuits, Y2K pieces (Von Dutch, Juicy, Ed Hardy, True Religion)
8. Structured blazers (wool, quality maker), COS, minimalist quality labels
9. Leather + suede jackets (condition-dependent)
10. Zara / Urban Outfitters standouts (filler tier, fast turns)

**Never buy:** fast fashion basics (Shein/Primark), pilled knits, stained whites, broken
zippers (unless grail-tier), anything you wouldn't photograph proudly.

## 3. Quality gates (60-second item check)

armpits → collar → cuffs → zipper → seams → smell → label. One strike on
stain/odor/damage = leave it, unless item is top-3 tier and flaw is disclosed-and-priced.

## 4. Weekly sourcing cadence

- **1 bin-store trip/week** (2–3 hrs): target 30–50 items, budget $60–100
- **1 thrift circuit/week** (2 hrs, 2–3 stores): target 10–15 curated pieces, budget $60–80
- **Month 2+:** 1 wholesale bale/month (45lb Y2K or denim mix, ~$200–350) → 60–90 items
- Every haul gets logged into `tools/inventory_tracker.csv` **the same day** with SKU + cost

## 5. Comp-checking protocol (do this BEFORE buying anything >$5)

Search the item on Depop + eBay **sold/completed** listings. Price against *sold*, never
*asking*. Conservative comp = the 25th percentile of recent solds in comparable condition.
(Claude runs deeper comp research for any item photographed and sent over.)

## 6. Landed-cost targets by phase

| Phase | Blended COGS/item ceiling | Target ASP | Ratio |
|---|---|---|---|
| M1–3 | $6 | $30 | 5x |
| M4–8 | $7 | $40 | 5.7x |
| M9–12 | $8 | $50–60 | 6–7x |

If blended COGS creeps above ceiling for two consecutive weeks → pause thrift, shift budget
to bins/bales until ratio recovers.
