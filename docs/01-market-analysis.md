## 2. Platform economics comparison (US, 2026)

| Platform | Seller fee | Effective take on $40 sale | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Depop** | 0% + 3.3% + $0.45 processing | **~4.4%** | Best economics in fashion resale. Optional boosted listings: pay 8–12% extra only if a boost causes the sale |
| eBay | ~13.25% + $0.30 | ~14% | Biggest buyer pool, best for menswear/vintage/denim |
| Mercari | ~10% + processing | ~13% | Fast casual sales, lower ASP |
| Poshmark | 20% flat (>$15) | 20% | Highest fee but strong for women's brands, social selling |

Strategy implication: **Depop is home base** (fee advantage + brand-building + young buyers).
Cross-list everywhere, but steer buyers to Depop when possible and let high-fee platforms
carry price-inflated listings (price +15–20% on Poshmark to net the same).

## 3. What's selling on Depop in 2026 (trend assessment)

From Depop's own 2026 trend forecast + seller data:

**Durable, high-velocity categories (core inventory):**
- **Y2K** — still the highest-volume search cluster; baggy skater jeans, vintage tracksuits
- **Workwear/streetwear staples** — Nike and Carhartt are the fastest-selling brands
- **Vintage denim** — always liquid; Levi's 501s are near-currency
- **Band/graphic tees** — high margin, light to ship

**Rising 2026 trends (buy ahead of these):**
- **"Minimalist Renaissance"** — searches +803%; the "Edited Self" shift toward fewer,
  better pieces. COS search interest +360%, Phoebe Philo +200%
- **Barn jackets** — searches +3,138% (biggest single riser)
- **Structured blazers, cashmere knits** — cashmere comps **$60–120/piece** on resale;
  it's routinely mispriced at $5–8 in thrift/bins (check labels obsessively)
- Trending labels: Cecilie Bahnsen, Conner Ives, Oakley (vintage sport), Prada Linea
  Rossa, Martine Rose — grail-tier when found

**Mall brands with resale premium:** Zara and Urban Outfitters fetch above-average prices
relative to source cost — good filler inventory from bins.

## 4. Seller-behavior data (what the winners do)

- Sellers listing on **3+ marketplaces: +180% sell-through** vs fewer
- Sellers listing **20+ items/month: 2x sell-through** vs <10/month
- Sellers who **relist: 7.5x more items sold** over 12 months vs never-relisters
- Top accounts post **4–6 new items daily** and have built 30k+ followings
- Bumped/refreshed listings get **40–60% more views** in the following 24h
- Top sellers earn **3–7x more revenue per listing** than platform average via tags,
  refresh cycles, and pricing discipline; top shops clear **$10k+/month**

## 5. Competitive positioning

Most Depop shops are: (a) closet-cleaners with 30 random items, (b) grail dealers with high
prices and slow turns, or (c) high-volume shops with terrible curation. The open lane is
**high-volume + tightly curated + branded** — algorithmically favored (freshness + reliability
signals) and buyer-favored (followable, repeat-purchase shop). That's the lane this plan takes.
