# 11 — Sourcing Map: San Mateo (now) → Tucson (soon)

The move is a strategic asset, not a disruption. Bay Area = the highest-quality donation
pool in the US (wealthy zips → cashmere, designer, quality labels cheap). Tucson = one of
the cheapest cost-of-living sourcing cities + a nationally hot niche (western/southwestern
vintage) at the source. **Phase the strategy around the move.**

## PHASE A — San Mateo / Peninsula (until the move)

**Mission: stockpile PREMIUM inventory while you have access to rich-zip donations.**
Peninsula donations come from Hillsborough/Atherton/Menlo Park/Burlingame — some of the
wealthiest zips in America. This is Pole B (cashmere, blazers, quiet luxury) heaven.
Every quality piece bought here travels with you and sells from anywhere.

### The bins (by-the-pound — volume days)
| Spot | Where | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| SF Goodwill Clearance Center | **100 Utah Ave, South San Francisco** (~15 min up 101) | Bins by the pound. Hours: M–F 11am–3pm, Sa–Su 9am–3pm. Go at OPEN; bins rotate through the day |
| Goodwill Clearance Center Oakland | 1301 30th Ave, Oakland | Bigger; worth 1–2 trips/month across the bridge |

### Thrift circuit (picked pieces — curation days)
1. Goodwill San Mateo — 28 W 25th Ave (your home store; learn its restock day)
2. Savers Redwood City + Thrift Center San Carlos (big floors)
3. **Church/community thrifts in Burlingame, San Carlos, Menlo Park** — small shops in
   rich towns are the single best cashmere-per-hour hunting in the country; volunteers
   price by "it's a sweater, $6," not by label
4. St. Vincent de Paul / Salvation Army SF (bigger swings, up 101)

### Estate sales (premium tier)
estatesales.net alerts for: Hillsborough, Atherton, Menlo Park, Burlingame, San Mateo
Park. Go final day (50–75% off). Target: cashmere, leather, silk scarves, vintage denim,
barn/field jackets, quality menswear.

## PHASE B — Tucson (after the move)

**Mission: volume at the cheapest COGS in the country + own the western-vintage niche.**
Lower rent = garage inventory room (the Engine 3 grading floor). Snowbird/retiree estates
= decades-deep closets: western wear, turquoise-era pieces, leather, Levi's, Pendleton.

### The bins
| Spot | Where | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Goodwill Outlet (Southern AZ) | **1940 E. Silverlake #405, Tucson** · 520-618-0067 | By-the-pound blue bins, rotated daily. Cheaper than Bay Area bins |
| Speedway outlet | Speedway Blvd | Local-favorite second bins option |

### Thrift circuit
1. Goodwill of Southern Arizona — **13 stores**; rotate 3–4 best (learn restock days)
2. **4th Avenue vintage district** — Tucson Thrift Shop, Buffalo Exchange (born in
   Tucson) — study their pricing as your retail comp ceiling; Buffalo Exchange is also a
   *sell/trade channel* for mid-tier stock
3. Golden Goose Thrift Shop (Catalina/upscale donor base), White Elephant (Green Valley
   — retiree gold), Value Village/Savers
4. Estate sales: estatesales.net alerts for Tucson foothills, Green Valley, Oro Valley —
   retiree estates are western-vintage motherlodes

### The Tucson niche play (this is the edge)
**Western/southwestern vintage is nationally hot and locally abundant:** vintage Wranglers
and Levi's, western shirts (pearl snaps), Pendleton, leather/suede, boots, concho belts,
southwestern knits. Coastal buyers pay 4–8x Tucson thrift prices. luckypull adds a third
pole: **desert/western vintage** — sourced at the source, sold to the coasts. Also: U of A
= 40k students for local Y2K demand and buyer liquidity.

## Move-week protocol
Inventory travels (it's the business). Before the move: 1 final estate-sale sweep of the
rich Peninsula zips + 1 last SSF bins run. Photograph everything BEFORE packing; keep
listing through the move (Depop doesn't know you're in a U-Haul — just set accurate
ship times).
