# 12 — The Seven-Figure Blueprint (consolidated master plan)

The definitive document. Everything in docs 00–11 rolls up into this.

## Part 1 — Is it possible? (base rates, not vibes)

- Seven-figure resale companies demonstrably exist: documented $450k+/yr individual
  resellers, $5–20k/week vintage live sellers, and the wholesale/rag-house layer above
  them doing more. The market is $29.7B online → $48.3B by 2030. Room is not the issue.
- The base rate for *anyone who starts* is low — because ~90% of starters never pass
  200 listings, never cross-list, and quit in the week 3–6 dip. The base rate for people
  who run doc-05's system for 24+ months is dramatically better. Survival + system IS
  the edge; the four engines are how survivors get to $1M+.
- The math has no magic step: $1M/yr = $2,740/day = e.g. 20 retail sales at $55 + two
  $600 live streams + one $400 wholesale bundle, daily average. Every component is
  something real operators already do.

**Verdict: possible, 3–4 year build, ~$60–100k profit in year 1 while building it.
Not possible: skipping the apprenticeship. The gate to seven figures is 24 months of
compounding without breaking the chain.**

## Part 2 — The gatekept part: seven-figure-NATIVE from day one

You can't skip stages, but you can build every stage on seven-figure rails so nothing
gets rebuilt later. Most sellers retrofit these at year 2 and lose a year. We do them
in month 1–3 while small:

1. **Entity + books from sale #1.** Sole prop → LLC at ~$2k/mo profit; S-corp election
   at ~$60k/yr profit (saves ~$5–8k/yr self-employment tax). Separate bank account DAY
   ONE. Every number in the ledger. A company you could sell has books; a hustle doesn't.
2. **Data as an asset.** The SKU ledger isn't admin — it's the moat. 12 months of
   item-level cost/price/days-to-sale data = a proprietary pricing model for OUR shop
   that no competitor has. AI compounds exactly as fast as this dataset grows.
3. **Audience capture from the first package.** Every order: insert card → IG/TikTok
   follow + "text LUCKY for 10% off" (SMS list via free tier tools early). The Engine 4
   list starts at order #1, not year 2. 1,500 true buyers on SMS ≈ a $20–40k/yr channel.
4. **Content flywheel from week 1.** 3–5 shorts/wk (haul → priced, sold-this-week,
   styling). Every follower earned now multiplies every future engine: retail buyers,
   live-stream viewers, and eventually wholesale customers all come from the same audience.
5. **Supply relationships before you need them.** From month 3: introduce yourself to
   2 rag houses / wholesalers per month (Claude drafts outreach). By month 12 when
   Engine 3 unlocks, you're a known buyer with negotiated terms, not a stranger.
6. **Brand consistency contract.** One handle, one look, one voice (doc 07) across every
   platform from day one — because the seven-figure endgame is the BRAND being worth
   more than the inventory.

## Part 3 — The staged build (gates, not dates)

Stages unlock on numbers, not calendar. Dates below are the aggressive-realistic pace.

### Stage 1 — ASSET (target: months 1–9) · goal $5k/mo revenue run-rate
Do: docs 08/05 loop — 5 listings/day, cross-list at month 3, 400+ active listings,
audience capture running, books clean.
Gate to Stage 2: 40 sales/wk · 5k followers · 200+ filler backlog · ledger shows
which categories are OURS (top-quartile sell-through).
Failure mode: quitting in the dip; breaking the daily chain. Counter: the quota is
sacred; everything else can flex.

### Stage 2 — MULTIPLY (months ~10–18) · goal $15–30k/mo
Do: 2–3 live streams/wk (Whatnot/TikTok Shop) clearing 40–100 items each; first
shipping help at 40+ shipments/wk; premium mix to 40%; SMS list past 1,000.
Gate to Stage 3: buying 1,000+ lbs/mo · grading 60+ items/hr · $15k/mo revenue
for 3 consecutive months.
Failure mode: live selling with weak inventory depth (dead streams). Counter: streams
launch only when backlog >200 items.

### Stage 3 — UPSTREAM (year 2–3) · goal $40–70k/mo
Do: buy unsorted at $0.50–2/lb direct from textile recyclers (Tucson/border region);
keep top 10% (pick cost → ~$0); wholesale graded middle in 50-item bundles at $4–6/lb
to smaller resellers; hire picker/grader; garage → small warehouse space.
Gate to Stage 4: wholesale channel repeating monthly · 10k+ audience · 2 staff stable.
Failure mode: bad bulk buys. Counter: never contract before a 500lb pilot lot with
measured pick-rates (Claude runs the math on every lot).

### Stage 4 — OWN (year 3–4) · goal $85–110k/mo = seven figures
Do: Shopify + SMS drops for premium tier (zero fees); 4 engines balanced; 2–3 staff;
operator hours = buying, brand, partnerships only.
Mature P&L: ~$1.3M revenue → $390–450k net.

## Part 5 — Kill criteria & pivot rules (honesty clause)

- 6 months of true execution (quota actually hit) with <$1,500/mo revenue → full
  strategy autopsy: niche, pricing, photos re-benchmarked against top shops.
- 12 months with <$3k/mo profit despite execution → we take the operating skills +
  audience + AI system and redeploy into the adjacent higher-yield model (B2B/AI
  services stack, doc discussion) — the skills transfer; sunk cost doesn't rule us.
- Any engine below 15% net margin for a quarter → cut or fix, no sentiment.
