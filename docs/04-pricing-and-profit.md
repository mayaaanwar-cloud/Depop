# 04 — Pricing & Profit Strategy

## 1. Fee reality per platform (what you actually keep)

On a $40 item + buyer-paid shipping (run `tools/profit_calc.py` for any price):

| Platform | Fees | You keep (before COGS) |
|---|---|---|
| Depop US | 3.3% + $0.45 | **$38.23** |
| eBay | ~13.25% + $0.30 | ~$34.40 |
| Mercari | ~10% + 2.9% + $0.50 | ~$34.34 |
| Poshmark | 20% | $32.00 |

**Cross-platform pricing rule:** set the Depop price as base; list +10% on eBay/Mercari and
+18–20% on Poshmark so net proceeds are roughly equal everywhere. (Vendoo applies per-platform
price rules automatically.)

## 2. Initial pricing formula

1. Comp against **sold** listings (Depop + eBay completed).
2. Price at the **60th–70th percentile of solds** — slightly above median, leaving offer room.
3. Floor: never list below **3x COGS + $5**.
4. Psychological points: $24, $32, $38, $48, $58 outperform round numbers; keep most items
   under free-shipping-threshold bundles rather than discounting price.

## 3. Offers & negotiation (Depop is an offer culture)

- **Likes = leads.** Send offers to likers within 24h: **10–15% off**, framed as expiring.
- Counter every lowball once at your floor; never insult-reply. Script bank in doc 05.
- **Bundle discount always on** (10% for 2+, 15% for 3+): raises average order value and
  amortizes shipping — bundles are the cheapest ASP raiser available.

## 4. Markdown & relist cadence (inventory aging discipline)

| Age | Action |
|---|---|
| Day 0–14 | Full price; send liker offers |
| Day 15–30 | Refresh listing; −10% offer wave to all likers |
| Day 30 | −15% price cut + **full relist** (delete/repost as new) |
| Day 60 | −25% cumulative; consider boost (8–12% fee acceptable on aging stock) |
| Day 90 | Bundle-bait pricing or lot it (sell as 5-item mystery/lot on eBay); free the capital |

Dead inventory is a silent killer — $8 stuck in a shirt for 6 months is $8 not compounding
through 3–4 flip cycles. **Sell-through > sunk pride.**

## 5. Margin guardrails

- Target blended net margin: **≥ 65%** of sale price after fees/COGS/packaging
- Any category averaging <50% net for 4+ weeks gets cut from the buy list
- Returns/problem reserve: budget 3% of revenue; refund fast and move on (reviews are an
  algorithm input — a $30 refund protecting a 5-star average is cheap)

## 6. ASP ladder (the quiet path to six figures)

Raising ASP $30 → $50 at constant volume is +67% revenue for zero extra shipping labor.
Mix shift by phase:

| Phase | Filler (<$25) | Core ($25–50) | Premium ($50–120) | Target ASP |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| M1–3 | 40% | 50% | 10% | $30 |
| M4–8 | 25% | 50% | 25% | $40 |
| M9–12 | 15% | 45% | 40% | $50–60 |

Premium tier = cashmere, Carhartt outerwear, barn jackets, leather, vintage Nike outerwear,
structured blazers, grail Y2K — exactly the doc 01 risers. The sourcing playbook feeds this
ladder deliberately.
