# 00 — MASTER PLAN: The Six-Figure Reselling Business

*Last updated: July 2026*

## 1. The opportunity, in numbers

- Global secondhand market: **$393B by 2030**, growing ~9%/yr (ThredUp 14th Annual Resale Report, 2026)
- US secondhand apparel: **$78.8B by 2030**; grew ~4x faster than new-clothing retail in 2025
- Online resale specifically: **$29.7B (2025) → $48.3B (2030)** — 10% CAGR
- Gen Z + Millennials drive **71% of all growth** through 2030 — exactly Depop's user base
- Depop US fee structure: **0% commission + 3.3% + $0.45 processing** — the lowest take-rate
  in fashion resale (Poshmark takes 20%, Mercari ~13% effective, eBay ~13.25%+)

The tailwind is real. The platform economics are the best they've ever been. The constraint
is not demand — it is **operator throughput**: how many well-chosen, well-listed items you
can push through the pipeline per week.

## 2. The business model

**Curated volume reselling.** Not pure thrift-flipping (too low ASP), not pure grail-hunting
(too slow, too capital-intensive). The model that actually compounds:

1. **Source cheap, in bulk** — bins ($1.50–2.50/lb), wholesale vintage ($2–5/lb), thrift
   ($4–8/item for picked pieces). Blended cost of goods target: **≤ $7/item**.
2. **Curate into a brand** — one aesthetic universe (doc 07), so followers buy repeatedly
   and the shop ranks as "reliable seller" in Depop's algorithm.
3. **List at volume** — 4–6 new listings/day minimum. Listing volume is the single highest-
   leverage activity: sellers listing 20+/month have **2x the sell-through** of those under 10.
4. **Cross-list everything** — Depop + eBay + Poshmark + Mercari via Vendoo (~$30/mo).
   3+ marketplaces = **+180% sell-through**.
5. **Feed the algorithm** — refresh/relist cycles, full tag slots, fast replies, same-day
   ship. Relisters sell **7.5x more** than non-relisters.
6. **Raise ASP over time** — start at ~$30 average sale price, climb to $45–60 by shifting
   mix toward the winning categories (doc 01: Y2K, Carhartt/Nike workwear-street, barn
   jackets, cashmere knits, minimalist "quiet" pieces — cashmere alone comps $60–120).

## 3. Unit economics (the math that runs everything)

Base case per item, Depop US, mid-phase (verify live with `tools/profit_calc.py`):

| Line | Amount |
|---|---|
| Sale price (ASP target, month 4+) | $40.00 |
| Buyer-paid shipping | pass-through |
| Depop processing (3.3% + $0.45) | −$1.77 |
| COGS (blended) | −$7.00 |
| Packaging | −$0.50 |
| Misc (returns/refunds reserve, 3%) | −$1.20 |
| **Net profit per sale** | **≈ $29.50** |

At that margin (~74% — this is why resale beats almost any retail business):

| Net profit goal | Sales needed/yr | Sales/day | Realistic? |
|---|---|---|---|
| $30,000/yr | ~1,000 | ~2.8 | Yes — part-time, months 1–8 |
| $60,000/yr | ~2,000 | ~5.6 | Yes — full effort + cross-listing, months 9–15 |
| $100,000/yr | ~3,400 | ~9.3 | Yes at $40 ASP with 4 platforms + help; **or** ~5.5/day at $60 ASP |

**The two levers to six figures are ASP and platform count.** At a $60 ASP (achievable with
curated vintage/workwear/cashmere mix), $100k profit = 5–6 sales/day across four
marketplaces — which top sellers demonstrably hit. Volume without ASP growth means grinding
9+ sales/day; ASP growth without volume means slow capital turns. Do both.

## 4. Capital plan

| Phase | Investment | What it buys |
|---|---|---|
| Launch (month 1) | $300–500 | First 60–100 items (thrift + one 45lb wholesale bale), poly mailers, tape, hanging rack, phone tripod |
| Growth (months 2–4) | Reinvest 50% of profit | Inventory to 300+ active listings, Vendoo subscription, better lighting |
| Scale (months 5–12) | Reinvest 40% | Inventory to 800–1,200 active listings, bulk bale contracts, shelving/SKU bins |

No debt. Inventory is the flywheel: every $7 item that sells returns ~$30 to buy 4 more items.
A 50% reinvestment rate roughly **doubles active inventory every 6–8 weeks** in the growth phase.

## 5. Why most sellers fail (and how this plan doesn't)

| Failure mode | Our counter |
|---|---|
| List 10 items, wait, get discouraged | Non-negotiable 4–6 listings/day quota; sell-through is a volume game |
| Random inventory, no brand | Brand bible (doc 07); one aesthetic; repeat buyers |
| One platform | Cross-list to 4 from month 2 |
| Bad photos | Fixed photo formula (doc 03); 20 min per batch of 10, not 20 min per item |
| No numbers | Inventory ledger + weekly review (doc 05); every item has a SKU and a cost |
| Stale listings rot | 30-day markdown + relist cadence (doc 04) |
| Overpaying for inventory | Hard ceiling: never pay >25% of conservative resale comp |

## 6. Risk register

- **Platform risk** (Depop changes fees/algorithm): mitigated by 4-platform spread + owned
  audience (Instagram/TikTok) from day one.
- **Trend risk** (Y2K fades): mitigated by mix — workwear, minimalist, cashmere, and denim
  are durable; monitor Depop's trend reports quarterly (Claude's job).
- **Time risk** (operator burnout): batching + the doc 05 workflows cap ops at ~3–4 hrs/day
  until profit funds a shipping VA (~month 10).
- **Cash risk**: no debt, 50% reinvestment cap, keep 1 month of expenses liquid.
- **Tax**: set aside 25–30% of profit from sale one; track everything in the ledger
  (1099-K threshold reporting applies — talk to a CPA at ~$20k revenue).

## 7. The 12-month headline (detail in doc 06)

- **M1–2:** Launch. 150 listings live. First $1.5–3k revenue. Learn the photo/list pipeline.
- **M3–4:** Cross-list. 350+ listings. $4–6k/mo revenue. ASP climbing past $35.
- **M5–8:** Scale sourcing (bale contracts). 700+ listings. $8–12k/mo revenue.
- **M9–12:** Optimize + delegate shipping. 1,000+ listings. **$12–18k/mo revenue run-rate**
  → six-figure revenue run-rate, ~$70–110k annualized profit at target margins.

Everything above is falsifiable — the weekly review (doc 05) checks actuals against this
plan and we adjust monthly.
