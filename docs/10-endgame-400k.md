# 10 — Endgame: The $400K-Profit Architecture

$400k/yr **profit** = a $1.2–1.6M revenue company at scale margins (25–35% net after
payroll/space/software; the solo 75% margin doesn't survive scaling). No single channel
gets there. Four stacked engines sharing one inventory pipeline do.

## The industry's real money map (the gatekept part)

At $400k+ in secondhand fashion, nobody is listing items one at a time. The money is:
1. **Upstream** — rag houses/wholesalers selling $2/lb goods at $5/lb to retail resellers
2. **Live** — Whatnot/TikTok Shop sellers clearing 40–150 items per 2-hour stream
3. **Owned** — brands with email/SMS lists paying zero marketplace fees

Depop retail is the apprenticeship (comps, grading speed, reputation, audience seed) —
not the destination.

## The four engines

| Engine | What | Starts | Mature revenue | Role |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Marketplace retail | Depop + eBay/Posh/Mercari (docs 00–09) | Day 1 | ~$300k/yr | Margin, reputation, comp knowledge |
| 2. Live selling | Whatnot/TikTok Shop, 2–3 streams/wk | ~M10 | ~$500k/yr | Volume; clears filler tier; fastest audience growth |
| 3. B2B supply | Buy unsorted $0.50–2/lb from recyclers; keep top 10% for engines 1–2; wholesale graded middle 60% in 50-item bundles at $4–6/lb | Y2–3 | ~$300k/yr | Zero-listing-labor volume; pick cost → ~$0 |
| 4. Owned channel | Shopify + email/SMS from every package insert & stream | Y2–3 | ~$200k/yr | Fee-free premium sales; makes the company sellable |

**Mature P&L (Y3–4):** ~$1.3M revenue → $390–450k net with 2–3 staff
(picker/grader, shipper, stream assistant).

## Engine unlock criteria

- **Engine 2 unlock:** 40+ sales/wk retail + 5k followers + filler backlog >200 items
- **Engine 3 pilot:** buying 1,000+ lbs/mo AND grading ≥60 items/hr accurately;
  pilot = one 500lb unsorted lot, track pick-rate % and per-tier yield before contracting
- **Engine 4 unlock:** 10k combined audience OR 500 past buyers on the insert-card list
- **First hire:** consistently 40+ shipments/wk (shipping first, always)

## AI's job at scale (extends doc 09)

Live-show run sheets & lot manifests · bale/lot bid math (cost/lb → pick-rate → landed
cost/tier → margin) · wholesale catalog + B2B outreach copy · email/SMS calendar ·
daily content scripts · cross-engine weekly capital allocation review (move inventory
$ to the highest-yielding engine, kill the lowest quartile).

## Timeline (honest)

| Year | Revenue | Profit | State |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | $80–150k | $60–100k | Engines 1–2; solo + AI |
| 2 | $350–600k | $150–250k | Engine 3 pilot; first part-time hires |
| 3–4 | $1.2M+ | **$400k+** | Four engines; 2–3 staff; owned audience |

Reliable beats fast: skipping the Y1 apprenticeship = buying bad bales blind, mispricing,
and dying at month 8. Every doc-08 rep trains the grading eye Engine 3 monetizes.

## Risk spread at maturity

Four engines = no single point of failure: a Depop fee change, a Whatnot policy shift,
or a supply hiccup each hits ≤ ~35% of revenue. The owned list (Engine 4) is the
uncancellable asset.
