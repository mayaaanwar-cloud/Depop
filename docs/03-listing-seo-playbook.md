# 03 — Listing, Photography & SEO Playbook

## 1. How the Depop algorithm ranks you (2026)

Three ranking signals, plus a multiplier:

1. **Relevance** — keywords in title/description + all hashtag slots filled
2. **Freshness** — recency of listing/refresh; new listings get a discovery window
3. **Engagement** — likes/saves/comments in the **first hour** after listing
4. **× Seller reliability** — consistent sales, fast replies, strong reviews give a baseline
   boost to *every* listing you post

Tactical consequences:
- **List daily (4–6 items), not weekly in batches** — daily freshness > one big dump
- **List at peak hours** (7–10pm local, Sun–Wed strongest) so the first-hour engagement
  window lands when buyers are online
- Answer messages fast (same hour if possible) — reliability is a ranking input
- **Refresh** older listings for a bump (refreshed listings: +40–60% views next 24h);
  after 30 days stale, **full relist** (delete + repost) — Depop treats it as new inventory,
  which is a stronger signal than a refresh. Update price/description when you do.
- Keyword-optimized listings rank measurably higher (~15% improvement) — never post a
  lazy title.

## 2. Photography formula (batch 10 items in ~30 min)

Fixed setup so every photo is on-brand and zero decisions are needed:
- Natural light near a window OR two softbox/ring lights; same wall/backdrop every time
- Phone on tripod, same height/distance, portrait orientation
- **Shot list per item (4–5 photos):** ① full front (flat-lay or mannequin/self-model —
  modeled photos convert best on Depop) ② back ③ label + size tag ④ detail/texture or
  styling shot ⑤ any flaw, honest close-up
- Steam/lint-roll before shooting. Consistent edit preset (same brightness/warmth) = the
  grid looks like a brand, not a garage sale.

## 3. Title formula

`[Brand] [Era/Style] [Item type] [Color] [Standout keyword] [Size]`

Examples:
- `Carhartt vintage chore coat barn jacket brown duck canvas workwear M`
- `Y2K baggy skater jeans wide leg grunge low rise dark wash 32`
- `100% cashmere crewneck sweater minimalist oatmeal knit quiet luxury L`

Front-load the highest-volume search terms. No emoji in titles, no "😍MUST HAVE😍" — search
relevance only.

## 4. Description formula (see `templates/listing-template.md`)

Line 1: one-sentence hook restating key search terms naturally.
Then: measurements (pit-to-pit, length, waist flat — ALWAYS), condition grade with flaw
disclosure, fabric, era, styling suggestion ("styles well with…" — adds long-tail keywords).
Close: shop plug ("bundle for a discount — new drops daily").

## 5. Tags/hashtags

Use **every available slot, every time**. Mix: 2 brand tags + 2 category + 2 style/aesthetic
+ era + size-adjacent terms. Pull the aesthetic vocabulary from doc 01 trends (y2k, gorpcore,
workwear, quiet luxury, minimalist, barn jacket, grandpacore, etc.) — only tags that are
true for the item; misleading tags hurt the relevance signal and cause returns.

## 6. Boosted Listings policy

US boost fee: pay 8–12% of sale **only when a boost causes the sale**. Rules:
- Never boost a weak listing — boosting amplifies, it doesn't fix
- Boost only: items >45 days old with good engagement but no sale, and proven winners in
  categories where we hold depth
- Check margin first: a $40 sale at 12% boost still nets ~$25 — acceptable on aging stock,
  not on fresh drops

## 7. The daily listing quota

**4–6 new listings per day, 7 days/week.** This is the single non-negotiable of the whole
business (20+/month = 2x sell-through; top shops do 4–6/day). Batch it: photograph
Sun/Wed (2 × 30-item shoots), then listing is 10 min/day of copy-paste from prepared
Claude-written copy blocks.
