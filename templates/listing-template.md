## Tags — use EVERY slot
2× brand · 2× category (`chorecoat`, `workwearjacket`) · 2× aesthetic (`y2k`, `workwear`,
`quietluxury`, `barnjacket`, `minimalist`, `gorpcore`) · era (`90s`, `vintage`) · fit/size
adjacent (`oversized`, `baggy`)

Only tags that are TRUE for the item.
