# Haul Intake Sheet (fill while photographing — 60 sec/item)

Paste completed rows to Claude → receive ready-to-list copy + prices same day.

Per item, one line, pipe-separated:

`SKU | Brand | Item type | Era/style guess | Color | Marked size | Pit-to-pit " | Length " | Waist/inseam (if pants) | Fabric (from tag) | Condition + ANY flaws | Your cost | Anything special`

## Example batch

```
A012 | Carhartt | chore coat | 90s? | duck brown | L | 24 | 31 | - | cotton duck | good, small paint fleck left cuff | 2.40 | union-made tag
A013 | Levi's | 501 jeans | vintage | dark wash | 34x32 tag | - | - | 17 flat / 32 | 100% cotton | very good, honest fade | 3.10 | made in USA
A014 | no brand | crewneck sweater | minimalist | oatmeal | M | 21 | 26 | - | 100% cashmere!! | excellent | 1.90 | 2-ply, heavy
```

## Rules
- Measure EVERY item laid flat (pit-to-pit + length minimum; waist flat + inseam for pants)
- Read the fabric tag every time — cashmere/wool/silk change the price 3–10x
- Note flaws honestly; Claude prices them in and discloses them
- If unsure of brand/era, photograph the tag close-up and say "tag unclear"
- Flag anything that feels special ("heavy", "single stitch", "talon zipper", "union tag")
  — these are exactly the details that make an item comp 2–5x higher
